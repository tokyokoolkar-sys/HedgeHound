# T13: building blocks + Edition skeleton (v0.3)

Branch `T13` (from main after T12).

## Owner decisions
- **1a:** the new Edition replaces `/`. The legacy home is parked at `/v1` until T51.
- **2b:** the new nav is on every page, so the legacy `components/nav.tsx` was deleted.

## What changed
- **`src/content/edition.ts`:** overture + α–κ, each with letter, kicker, title, subtitle, body, note, section length, enter mode (`crawl` / `rise` / `fade`), layout and anchor. Copy only, no figures (enforced by a test).
- **`src/lib/motion/choreo.ts`:** copy choreography as pure functions of progress (`copyFrame`, `resolveIn`, `crawlFront`). Titles resolve from blur, items rise in sequence, chapters blur out upward at the end, and `rise` chapters travel up from below the screen.
- **`components/edition/`**
  - `chapter.tsx`: registers the section(s) and puts the copy on the fixed stage.
    - Crawl chapters get a `:enter` section and are rebuilt by DOM bite masks.
    - `exitInto` carries the previous copy up and out during the next crawl, fading before it reaches the nav.
    - The overture is visible on first paint, including under reduced motion.
  - `shimmer-title.tsx`: `@spell/shimmer-text` on the hero / chapter / title roles.
  - `stardust.tsx`: text that gathers out of 2px specks, driven by a progress function.
  - `projected-label.tsx` + `lib/stage/projector.ts` + `components/stage/projector.tsx`: page DOM pinned to 3D points, positioned by SkyStage each frame. This replaces drei `<Html>` for anything refreshing.
  - `masks.ts`: the bite (`applyCrawl`) and stardust masks, ported from D1.
  - `hud.tsx`: Sirius' coordinate drifting with the pointer, moved to bottom-left because the nav owns the top (`lib/stage/coordinates.ts`, tested).
  - `chapter-index.tsx`: α–κ on the left edge, active letter lit cyan, click to jump, hidden under 768px.
  - `look.ts`, `use-scroll-frame.ts`: per-frame style writers, with no React state.
  - `edition.tsx`: the Edition. It asks for the sky, mounts the HUD, index and chapters, and maps section progress to scene channels (overture / α / crawl, from D1; T14 refines this).
- **`components/nav/site-nav.tsx`:** wordmark (subtitle step, thin display) and mono links straight on the sky, with no bar or glass. "Open scanner" comes with `/scanner` (T30/T31).
- **Routes:** `/` is now the Edition; `/v1` is the legacy home.
