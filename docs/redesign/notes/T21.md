# T21: δ The Trail

Branch `T21-trail`. Owner approved (a) draw honestly from live fields, (b) the comet is the loudest scent (same coin as β and γ).

## What landed
- **Comet trail** (`objects/comet-trail.tsx`, geometry in `lib/stage/comet.ts`): a faint 24-hour arc with six-hour ticks. The coin's star replays its day along it (scroll-locked) and lays the trail behind: solid in the current direction's colour since the last flip, dashed faint before it, nothing where there are no samples. Then a tick at the flip and a bracket over the last six hours. Built only from `window_h`, `hours_since_flip` and `persistence` (checked against `scanner/server.py`); no `trailFor()`.
- **Labels**: `−24 h` / `now` beside the ends, one right-aligned block under `now` ("last flip · 1.4 h ago" / "held 42% of the last 6 h"; "held all N h of samples" when there was no flip), caption `δ {symbol} · the last 24 h` with the SourceTag.
- **Copy**: what "stays" means (the two questions the scanner asks). Second beat "Six that stayed": the six steadiest coins as inline SVG strips drawn like the trail, mono shorthand under each, one SourceTag, screen-reader list.
- **Stacked (phones)**: the arc tucked under the copy, then the trail alone, then the strips.
- `segments()` is the single source for both the 3D trail and the strips; tested (held / before / no samples / no-flip case / day cap), plus arc geometry and labels.
