# T20: γ The Spread

Branch `T20-spread`. First P3 chapter, built on the β reference pattern. Owner approved (a) thin lines, (b) scroll-locked rotation, (c) the price swing.

## What landed
- **Astrolabe** (`objects/hedge-astrolabe.tsx`, geometry in `lib/stage/astrolabe.ts`): a cyan ring (long) inside an orange ring (short), 48 inward ticks each, around the coin's star. They counter-rotate with the scroll, slide apart into two legs (each with its own star), and a starlight hairline joins the stars. Then one price swing (up, down, rest) moves both legs together: the hairline never changes length. Tested: equal y for both legs at every swing step, constant gap, a ≥ 1.5-unit clear gap for the "spread" label, and the whole thing fits the layout box.
- **Labels** (`chapters/03-spread.tsx`): `long · {venue}` and `short · {venue}` from the live row, "spread" centred in the gap, the swing caption "price moves both legs · the gap stays", and a caption `γ {symbol} · both legs` with the SourceTag.
- **Copy**: the spread is finally defined ("…the gap between their hourly rates is the spread"), then three steps from the old cards (Short the rich hour, Long the cheap hour, If one leg fails). The fourth ("$20 transfers…") was dropped: unverified figure.
- **Live second beat**, "The scent, both legs": coin, long, short, net APR after costs, steady (persistence), since flip, history, one SourceTag. It is the same coin as β's reading (shared `pickLoudest`). Screen-reader copy of the live rows.
- **Stacked (phones)**: steps take turns under the definition with the rings tucked below; then the astrolabe alone (split, swing); then the live legs.
