## What landed
- **`routes/scanner.tsx` + `components/scanner/scanner-page.tsx`**: the operator's table straight on the sky. Title in the display face, "net after a 0.30% round trip at a 72 h hold" with a PAPER tag (the book's defaults), status words (all / clean / building), a hairline net-floor slider ("any" at the left end), "N of M coins" with the table's LIVE/SAMPLE tag.
- **Columns**: coin · net · gross · held 6 h ("shaky" under half) · samples n/144 · last flip (≥ window when it never flipped) · legs (cyan ↑ long, orange ↓ short) · liq dist · status (+ stale). Net is orange only when clean and paying. Header buttons sort (biggest first; A→Z for coin; freshest flip first) and flip on a second click, with `aria-sort`.
- **Layout**: the header sits outside the scrolling rows, so rows clip crisply at its hairline. Under 1024px each coin is two lines and sorting is a row of words; seven columns at 1024; gross and liq dist from 1280. Thin, faint scrollbar. Footer: how many more were skipped on purpose, with a link to η, and the Phase 0 line.
- **`lib/scan/table.ts`** (pure, 6 tests): parses `?status&min&sort&dir&sym` (invalid and default values dropped), filters, sorts, and keeps the pointed-out coin even when the filters would hide it.
- **Links**: a row (or its symbol link) opens the coin on the sky (`/universe?star=` until T33). ⌘K on /scanner points the coin out in the table (`?sym=`, cyan hairline, scrolled into view). Scanner joined the nav. ε's shortlist ends with "Open scanner →" (Beat gained an `action` slot: the only focusable, non-aria-hidden part, unreachable while the beat is hidden).
- **Quiet sky** (`QUIET_SCENE`, `useRequestSky("quiet")`): no instrument, no hound, no constellation, stars at 40%. Leaving the Edition mid-chapter no longer leaves its instruments in the sky.
- **Chapter links**: `/#chapter-<id>` lands where that chapter's copy has settled. `scrollToTarget` resizes Lenis before an immediate jump (it still held /scanner's zero scroll limit), and the Edition waits for Lenis on a fresh load.
