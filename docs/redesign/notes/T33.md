# T33: /sky (prototype stage)

Branch `T33-sky`. Owner approved a–e: the page, the list, routes and links, deleting the legacy view, and a prototype of both focus feels (`?fx=fly` switch, bottom right; under the header on phones). Not merged until the owner picks.

## What landed
- **`/sky`** (`routes/sky.tsx`, `components/sky/sky-page.tsx`): θ's atlas on the one canvas, real scan rows only. The field fits beside the list column (it compresses right as you arrive), background stars at 60%, θ's recap rings and Bayer letters, the five loudest labelled (three on phones), hover rings a star (and names it unless it is already named).
- **Focus** (click a star or a name, `?star=`, Esc / empty sky pulls back): a cyan ring closes on the coin, a leader line draws, the reading gathers out of specks (as β): status, symbol in the display face, net + SourceTag, gross, held 6 h (shaky), samples, last flip, legs, liq dist; "Open in scanner →" and "pull back · esc". Phones and portrait tablets dock the reading at the bottom.
  - **reading**: the field magnifies 1.6× around the coin and slides it beside the list.
  - **fly**: the camera travels into the field until the coin is 2.2 units away; every coin has its own depth (projected onto the same spot at rest, so θ and /sky match exactly), the background stars stream past.
  - Moving between coins: focus eases back part-way, travels, and comes in again, so the reading steps out instead of sliding across the screen.
- **Maths** (`lib/stage/atlas.ts`, 20 new tests): `focusTransform` (both modes, list inset, depth-correct landing), `atlasLocal`, `depthOf`, `focusTarget`; the atlas publishes every coin's world and screen point for labels and hit-testing.
- **θ → /sky**: "Enter the sky →" in θ's caption. Same field, same spots, same canvas: the atlas holds, θ's words leave, the list and labels rise in. Reduced motion: no tweens.
- **Routes**: `/universe` redirects to `/sky` keeping `?star`; `SKY_ROUTE` is `/sky` (nav, ⌘K, scanner rows, ledger, den links).
- **Deleted**: `components/universe-view.tsx`, `components/three/universe-scene.tsx` (SAMPLE-only: art-directed blurbs and sectors, seeded sparklines, a localStorage watchlist).
- Camera pointer drift fades while a coin is in focus (it was throwing fly's landing off); coins never pass under the list column.
