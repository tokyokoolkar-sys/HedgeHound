  - **Why nothing clears**: with no clean coin yet, ι's caption and /den say so ("no coin is clean yet: the scanner needs a day of samples"), not "lower your bar" (`emptyReason`).
  - **θ**: the field sits beside the title on wide screens (the same inset /sky uses, so θ → /sky no longer shifts it) and a little lower on phones; recap labels no longer run into the title.
