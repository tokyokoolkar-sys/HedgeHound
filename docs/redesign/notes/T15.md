# T15: β Sniff + radar (reference implementation)

Branch `T15-sniff`. Last ticket of P2 Foundation (v0.3).

## What landed
- **Radar** (`objects/radar.tsx`, layout in `lib/stage/radar.ts`): rings, 5°/30° bearing ticks, the cyan sweep with pings (the chapter's one ambient motion). Every axis means something (owner decision after the critique):
  - half = venue carrying the short leg (right = short Lighter, left = short Binance); bearing inside the half is a symbol hash, so a coin holds still across refreshes;
  - radius = signed net APR; outer ring = break-even; losing spreads sit just outside, dimmed, unlabelled;
  - alerting (clean) coins scent orange, clamped so they never overdrive to red.
- **Projected DOM** (`chapters/02-sniff.tsx`): coin labels (loudest positive spreads, no two touching, pointing inward), caption + key + SourceTag, half captions, and the reading (leader line, then star specks) docked right of the ring or below it.
- **Layout per viewport** (`radarLayout`): wide (reading right), mid 768–~1280 (reading below), stacked on phones and portrait tablets. Stacked = one thing at a time: steps over tucked rings → radar featured alone with the reading → the rules.
- **Copy**: Normalize / Cancel / Doubt as numbered steps (01–03), then a second beat, "What the scanner checks", with every threshold checked against `WhaleHunterAlt/scanner/server.py` (POLL 300, AVG_MIN_SAMPLES 144, MIN_LISTING_AGE_DAYS 30, MIN_VOL24_USD 250k, MIN_OI_USD 50k, zero-rate skip) and labelled "not readings".
- New primitives: `edition/beat.tsx` (second beat), `Stardust` respects hidden parents, scene mappers per chapter (`chapters/sniff-scene.ts`), `ChapterDef.steps / out / beat / size / stacked`.
